Part 1: Foundation & Strategy
1. Title & Investment Thesis
CausaLab: The Causal Multi-Omics Discovery Engine for Therapeutics

Subtitle: Building the first industrial platform that integrates causal gene regulatory networks, multi-omics fusion, and programmable synthetic biology into a closed-loop therapeutic discovery engine.

Investment Thesis:
CausaLab is positioned as the first therapeutics-first causal multi-omics discovery company, bridging the gap between phenotypic correlation (Recursion) and data-first ML (Insitro) by building an enterprise-scale causal inference engine. By decoding context-specific disease networks and linking them to programmable intervention layers, CausaLab will unlock a new class of causal therapeutic targets and interventions.

Platform Value: Capable of generating multiple therapeutic pipelines across oncology, neurodegeneration, and immunology.

Differentiation: Focus on causality → discovery + intervention, rather than correlation-driven AI.

Target Investor Profile: Series A investors focused on platform therapeutics, e.g., Flagship Pioneering, ARCH, a16z Bio + Health, GV.

Compute Fabric: Hybrid of NetDecoder (context networks), SIGNET (causal inference), MUUMI (meta-analysis), CORNETO (multi-sample optimization).

Wet-Lab Stack: Cell models + programmable phospho-circuits.

Assay Specs:

qPCR, RNA-seq, ATAC-seq, proteomics.

Validation: NGS + flow cytometry.

CROs for rare assays; in-house for transcriptome/proteome.

Governance: SOC-2, HIPAA, FDA 21 CFR Part 11.
