# Antimicrobial peptides -- Jan 17, 2025



Can we use large scale compendia of bacterial species RNA-seq data in the presence of antibiotics to identify modes of action of antibiotic killing to then identify proteins of interest to design antimicrobial peptides against? 



Can we leverage some data from how the human immune system responds to bacterial infection (at the bacteria side) to identify novel mechanisms of action for bacterial cell killing? —> is there literature on this? Have people tried this before?

## Challenges & counterpoints
- ⚡ [[How useful are zero-shot predictions of mutational effects?]] (2026-09-28 · clip) — Woolley et al. show zero-shot protein models fail specifically for new-to-nature function-enhancing mutations — exactly what peptide therapeutic design requires.
- ⚡ [[We still can’t predict much of anything in biology]] (2026-09-28 · clip) — BindCraft shows peptide design tools yield ~1% success in practice despite 10-100% claimed rates.
