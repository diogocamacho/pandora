## The one move each payday

Bills actually needs **$15,342/mo** but the 50% split only deposits **$13,117**, short **~$2,225/mo**. Cover it from Savings, on your two paychecks only:

> **On the 15th and the last day of each month: transfer $1,200 from Savings → Bills.**
> Allison's weekly deposit: nothing extra.

That's $2,400/mo into Bills, a ~$175/mo cushion over the need. **Let the cushion pile up in low-bill months (spring/summer) to cover the winter oil and electric spikes. Don't sweep the Bills balance to zero.**

## What Savings does right now: kill the cards

After the two $1,200 transfers, **~$2,847/mo stays in Savings.** All of it goes to the cards until they're gone.

- Order (avalanche): **JetBlue (29.74%) to zero, then Fidelity (17.49%).**
- Keep paying the card **minimums from Checking** as usual; the Savings money is the accelerated paydown on top.
- Expected payoff: **~5 months (cleared ~spring 2027)**, with no reliance on the bonus.
- Cash stays flat during this window. That's fine. Flat cash + $15k less debt = $15k richer, at a guaranteed 29.74%.

**When the cards hit $0, the Savings bucket flips to building the emergency fund** (cash floor $50,421, then on toward the $100k target). Same bucket, new job.

## No new card charges (until the cards are at $0)

Once you carry a balance, there's no grace period — new charges accrue interest from day one. So during payoff:

- Pull JetBlue & Fidelity out of Apple Pay, Amazon, DoorDash, your Apple ID, and browser autofill.
- Everyday spend → debit/Checking. Lumpy/planned spend → the lifestyle account.
- Keep ~$1,500 in the lifestyle account as the "this is the card now" buffer so a surprise never forces a swipe.

## Sunday 10-minute check

## Shrink the top-up over time

Every $100/mo trimmed from Bills (GEICO re-quote, Petro reconcile + budget plan, Verizon audit, landscaping) is $100 less to transfer and $100 more hitting the cards. Claw back ~$500/mo and the $1,200 transfer drops toward $700, and the payoff accelerates by about a month.
