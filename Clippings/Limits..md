## How Claude counts.

Claude counts in tokens. A token is a piece of a word.

Every message, Claude pays for 3 things:

1. **It reads.** Your whole conversation, from the top. Every message. Every file. Again.
2. **It thinks.** The effort setting decides how much.
3. **It writes.** The answer, the doc, the deck.

Also, Claude keeps a saved copy of what it already read. That’s the **cache**. It counts much less against your weekly limit.

Every rule below does one of three things:

→ Makes Claude think less.  
→ Keeps the conversation short.  
→ Protects the cache.

## The non-negotiables.

#### 1\. Use Medium effort. Never Max.

Click the model name next to the send button. Click **Effort**.

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!ksSu!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F43dadcb2-4d1b-4c7a-8a60-905a8bba0f90_2046x923.png)

Never ever use Max. Trust me.

Your level of effort determines (1) how smart the model is, here as an intelligence score and (2) how expensive it is to do a task (so how much of your tokens it will use).

You must use Medium, always:

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!qxNK!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fa845130c-5904-469a-84df-f334460d7dce_2047x1805.png)

Never Extra or Max. And Medium for most - if not everything.

The rule:

→ **Low** for grammar, reformatting and translations.  
→ **Medium** for everything else.  
→ **High** for the one-shot prompt for a super-ambitious work.  
→ **Never Extra. Never Max.**

#### 2\. Save your free reset for a bad day.

Go to **Settings → Usage**. Find the **Resets** section.

Anthropic gave subscribers one free reset.

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!olZX!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fd9fbf886-0525-4a37-b1f2-f044c81f4f0a_3456x2172.png)

It’s a one-time reset.

Never use it on a Monday morning. Your limits reset every Sunday.

Also, fun fact: every Sunday I go extra crazy with my Claude (using Extra or Max), so I use up the tokens until the limits. It resets at 5 pm.

#### 3\. Use Opus 5.5. Every chat. Never Fable.

On the $100 plan, Opus 5.5 gets you $5,725 worth of work a month. Fable 5.1 gets you $1,273.

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!f4pi!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fa034743d-e574-4bf3-a272-d332a4cb9f57_2046x1674.png)

Sonnet 5.5 also looks good, but Opus is slightly smarter.

If you need a quick answer, just use the lower effort. Never change the model.

#### 4\. Never change model between turns.

Each model has its own cache.

Switch from Opus to Sonnet mid-chat, and Claude re-reads the whole conversation at full price.

Pick Opus 5.5. Start the chat. Never switch.

#### 5\. Never come back to an old chat.

The cache expires when you take a break.

Come back hours later, and your next message pays for the whole conversation again. Even a one-line question.

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!ke0b!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F82292204-218f-43db-bfba-c11016a26aaa_2045x338.png)

Anthropic explained it. I read everything for you.

Before you leave a long chat, prompt:

Summarize this chat in 10 lines: what we decided, what’s still open, and the next step.

The next time you need it, open a new chat. Paste the summary. Continue.

#### 6\. End quick questions with “No web search. No files.”

The new Claude decides by itself when to search the web, run code, or create files. Those tasks use more of your limit.

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!phhK!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F68a0baf7-eb9b-4bbc-924b-9537270b244d_1784x320.png)

So for every quick question, add this line:

Answer here in the chat. No web search. No files. Under 150 words.

Use Research (type /deep-research) only when you need 20+ sources. Never for one.

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!-Iye!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F5e7d7f60-73ee-4c8b-8ce5-5f36ffbc3443_3456x1462.png)

on the + you have a toggle for Research

#### 7\. Plan in text. Ask for the file last.

Docs, Slides and Design eat your limit. A long doc “uses more of your limit than a typical message” (Anthropic).

Never open with “Make me a 12-slide deck.”

Plan the outline in plain text first. Agree on every section and the text inside the slides. Then:

Build this exact deck. Don’t change the outline.

#### 8\. Highlight. Never ask for a full redo.

When Claude writes a doc, it opens beside your chat.

Highlight the part you want changed. Click **Edit with Claude**. Type the change. Claude only rewrites what you marked.

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!DtwB!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F68fd51d9-9c5a-448e-9ede-1b48b53abd5a_3456x2166.png)

You can highlight the text on the right and only redo this.

In a plain chat, write:

Only redo this section. Keep everything else.

#### 9\. Load your apps on demand.

Every app you connect (Gmail, Drive, Notion, Slack...) takes up room in your chats.

Click the **+**. Then **Connectors → Tool access → On demand**. Claude only loads the apps it needs. Then switch off every app this chat doesn’t need.

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!zI4h!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Ff41d0324-a46e-4adb-b661-ad95d9febed7_3456x1604.png)

Toggle this, and only activate the necessary Connectors.

#### 10\. Paste these two sentences into your instructions.

Go to **Settings → General → Instructions for Claude**. Paste:

Once you have answered something, treat that answer as done. On later turns, focus your thinking on what the user is asking now, and don’t go back over an earlier answer unless the user asks about it or points out a problem with it.

They’re Anthropic’s words. In Anthropic’s tests: less thinking on follow-ups, faster replies, same quality.

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!5wu7!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fc0137f37-32bb-460b-9e84-cbb381df4aa0_1824x926.png)

Straight from Anthropic’s Opus 5.5 prompting guide.

Then delete every “think step by step” and “think hard” line you ever wrote. Effort does that job now. No need to prompt it.

#### 11\. Crop every screenshot. Convert every PDF.

A full screenshot costs up to 4,784 tokens. A tight crop can cost under 100.

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!yj2Y!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F4f9096a9-8570-4ac7-87f7-6398e3debd66_2047x1805.png)

Share small screenshots, not your entire screen.

A PDF page costs 1,500 to 3,000 tokens, plus the page as an image.

Convert every PDF to text before you upload it:

1. Type doc.new in your browser.
2. Paste the text you need.
3. File → Download → Markdown (.md).

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!ZU31!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F694893f8-ca6a-4378-8f52-7dc66a33bb88_1400x1500.png)

#### 12\. Pause every scheduled task you don’t read.

Scheduled tasks run in the cloud, even when your laptop is closed. Each run is a full task.

An hourly task = 24 runs a day. Every single day. A lot of tokens.

Click **Scheduled** in the left sidebar. Pause what you don’t read.

## The basics.

#### 13\. Start every task with “ask me questions.”

Never write a 500-word prompt. You never need 500 words.

Because it gets re-read with every turn.

Write this instead:

I want to \[task\] to \[success criteria\]. Ask me questions using AskUserQuestion before you start.

Claude asks multiple-choice questions. You click. Almost zero tokens.

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!YzCw!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Ffe3c4709-84af-47a6-b4d1-8b7db8433b87_3456x2168.png)

#### 14\. Put all your asks in one message.

Three messages = three full re-reads. One message with three asks = one.

So ask three things (or more) in one prompt:

Summarize this article, list the main points, and suggest a headline.

#### 15\. Speak. Don’t type.

Typed prompts are lazy: “make it better.” Then you send 5 corrections.

Spoken prompts carry the full picture in one shot.

Use Wispr Flow (a voice-to-text app) or the mic button in Claude.

Wispr Flow is much better though.

#### 16\. Never correct Claude. Start over.

Every “No, I meant...” stays in the chat forever. Claude re-reads it with every message. And it confuses Claude sometimes too.

When a chat goes sideways: open a new one. Paste a 3-line brief. Go.

Or my favorite feature: the edit.

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!usQe!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fc173edf7-5a44-4439-86f2-4a746a7527df_3456x460.png)

If you edit your prompt, the chat restarts from this point.

#### 17\. One topic per chat.

LinkedIn post, then client proposal, then dinner recipe. Same chat.

Never. New topic = new chat. Always.

Unless you want it to be a token vortex.

#### 18\. Keep your instructions under 2,000 words.

Your instructions, your project instructions, your about-me file (the file that tells Claude who you are): Claude reads them again and again.

Keep each one under 2,000 words. Put the details of today’s task in the chat.

#### 19\. Put every file you reuse in a Project.

Upload a file to 5 chats, and Claude reads it 5 times.

Upload it once to a Project. Anthropic: “Content in projects is cached and counts less against your limits when reused.”

In the desktop app, give Claude one folder. Only *one*. Never your whole Documents folder.

#### 20\. In Claude Code: one task, then /clear.

Give Claude Code one precise task:

Create a bar chart from this CSV showing monthly revenue for 2025. Save it as chart.png.

Then:

- Type **/clear** (it empties the chat) before the next task.
- Keep your CLAUDE.md (the file Claude Code reads first) under 200 lines.
- Type **/usage** once a week to see what eats your limit.

#### 21\. Split your day into 3 sessions.

Claude gives you a 5-hour window, plus a weekly limit.

Work in 3 blocks: morning, afternoon, evening. Check **Settings → Usage** before every big task.

#### 22\. Ask Claude for images. But rarely.

Claude can’t make images like photos.

But Claude can code an image. It just uses a lot of tokens (compared to the free ChatGPT image generator).
