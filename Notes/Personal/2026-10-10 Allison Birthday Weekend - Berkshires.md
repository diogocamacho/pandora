# Allison's Birthday Weekend: Berkshires (Oct 10-12, 2026)
Base: Hilton Garden Inn Lenox/Pittsfield, 1032 South St, Pittsfield (413-448-2222). Check-in 3pm, checkout 11am. Indoor pool, on-site Jae's Restaurant, free parking.
Crew: Allison (birthday Sun 10/11), Diogo, Pedro (13), Filipe (10).

## Sat 10/10 - Arrive + easy first day
- ~9:00 Leave Sudbury (about 2h15 via the Pike). Lunch on the way or in Pittsfield.
- ~1:00 Berkshire Museum (39 South St, Pittsfield; 10-5). Aquarium, art, science. New Egyptian animal mummification exhibit opens 10/10. Adult $18, youth 4-17 $8.
- 3:00 Check in, pool for the kids.
- Dinner: casual and close. Papa Joe's, The Roasted Garlic, or Zucchini's (pizza/Italian). Keep the big dinner for Sunday.
- Hancock Shaker Village closes at 4pm Sat (private event), so it moves to Sunday.

## Sun 10/11 - Allison's birthday
- Morning: slow start. Kids at the pool with Diogo so Allison gets a quiet hour with coffee and a book.
- 10:00 Hancock Shaker Village (Rt 20, 10-5). Barns/animals, hands-on crafts. Adults $20, 13-17 $8, 12 and under free. Allow 2 hrs.
- 12:30 Lunch, then Patriots vs Raiders, 1:00pm kickoff, home game. Watch at hotel or a pub (Patrick's Pub is an option). Confirm the pub shows it.
- Afternoon: downtime or a short foliage drive.
- ~6:30 Birthday dinner (book now): Mazzeo's (Italian), District Kitchen & Bar (downtown), or Hotel on North. Fallback: Jae's at the hotel. Ask for a dessert with a candle.

## Mon 10/12 - Foliage day (Indigenous Peoples' Day)
- 9:30 Check out and drive to Mount Greylock (~40 min). Summit road open through 10/30. War Memorial Tower open daily through Indigenous Peoples' Day, so Monday is its last day. Visitor center 9-12:30, 1-4.
- ~12:30 Lunch in North Adams.
- ~1:30 MASS MoCA (open Mon 10-5; closed Tuesdays). Adults $25, children 6-16 $10, under 6 free.
- ~3:30 Drive home via the Mohawk Trail (Rt 2) for more color. About 3 hrs to Sudbury. Skip MoCA if the kids are fading.

## Caveats
- Typical peak: northern Berkshires around the week of 10/13; central county (Pittsfield/Lenox) around late October. Expect early-to-patchy color in Pittsfield and better color at elevation and north (Greylock, North Adams). No 2026 forecast found; check the DCR foliage map the week of.
- Berkshire Museum's Arrowhead Fall Fest was Oct 11 in 2025. Check whether it repeats on 10/10 or 10/11 in 2026.
- Hours and prices are from official sites as of today. Call to confirm Hancock Shaker for Monday.
- Pack layers. Check the forecast.
