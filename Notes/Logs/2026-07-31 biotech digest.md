# 2026-07-31 biotech digest

## Top story

**FDA opened its review of Sandoz's generic tirzepatide.** The first real generic-GLP-1 pathway is now in motion. Once a low-cost tirzepatide is on the market, the GLP-1 user base widens fast, and every one of those users is a candidate for companion nutrition. This lands directly on [[💡 X2|X2]]'s GLP-1 Support thesis: cheaper access grows the addressable market for the exact product X2 is building, and it grows it sooner than a branded-only market would have.

## Other items

- **EU cleared AbbVie's Rinvoq in alopecia areata and non-segmental vitiligo** (age 12+, both approved July 30). First systemic therapy in Europe for non-segmental vitiligo. JAK inhibitors keep expanding into autoimmune skin disease — a reminder of how much runway immunology still has.
- **BMS raised full-year guidance on ~$13B Q2 revenue** (beat consensus by ~10.6%). Big-pharma balance sheets are flush, which keeps the M&A engine hot — relevant to the deal environment below.
- **2026 M&A already tops all of 2025:** ~$134B across 33 deals over $1B in H1. Pharma is buying pipeline and platforms aggressively; the seller's market favors differentiated assets.

## AI in biotech

- **Anthropic acquired Coefficient Bio for ~$400M** and **Insilico signed a $2.75B pact with Lilly** — AI-native discovery has moved from pitch-deck narrative to core deal rationale. Chai Discovery (structure prediction) and AdvanCell (radiopharma) also raised two of the year's top venture rounds.
- **So what for Abio:** the bar for "differentiated computational platform" keeps rising. When a general-AI company like Anthropic buys into wet-lab biology and Lilly pays $2.75B for a discovery engine, the market is repricing what counts as a defensible AI-design moat. Abio's edge has to be the active-learning loop and validated designed biologics, not "we use ML."

## Nutraceutical / DTC watch

- **Thorne keeps executing across X2's exact lanes:** Taia (AI wellness advisor) is live, the GLP-1 Support Stack is shipping, and they just launched Perimenopause Complete and Women's Libido Boost behind a national campaign with Misty Copeland and Lana Condor. That's AI personalization + GLP-1 companion + women's health, all moving at once — the closest competitive shadow over X2's roadmap.
- **"Companion nutrition" is now a named category** with 83% of GLP-1 users "very interested." The market is validating the bet and filling in around it at the same time.

## Signal for the week

Generic GLP-1 entering FDA review + incumbents racing to own companion nutrition = the GLP-1-companion window is opening fast and getting crowded at the same time. Validation and threat arrive together. X2's move is a differentiated wedge (formulation IP, the agentic personalization layer, a channel Thorne can't easily copy) staked now, while the category is still forming. Connects to Abio too: the AI-bio M&A repricing says platform differentiation is the whole game in both of Diogo's worlds this year.
