# 2026-07-07 biotech digest

## Top story
Two FDA decisions land today. **Vera Therapeutics' atacicept** (IgA nephropathy) and **Enhertu** (Daiichi/AZ) as post-neoadjuvant treatment for HER2+ early breast cancer. The Enhertu one matters most for the field — pushing an ADC earlier into the treatment paradigm expands the addressable population and keeps raising the bar for what "targeted biologic" TAM looks like.

## Other items
- **M&A:** Zymeworks acquiring Theravance Biopharma for ~$929M all-cash. H1 2026 dealmaking is at a record pace — public and private biotech investors getting rewarded.
- **Sector:** XBI rode a huge H1 (bounced from ~$66 in April toward ~$123 by late 2026 per recent data) on positive readouts in lung cancer, Alzheimer's, and autoimmune. Biotech risk appetite is strong — relevant to Diogo's XBI exposure.
- **Autoimmune momentum:** atacicept decision + the broader autoimmune readout streak signals capital and attention rotating into immunology.

## Nutraceutical / DTC watch
- **Thorne launched "Taia," an AI Wellness Advisor** that answers questions like "what supplements should I take on a GLP-1?" plus a GLP-1 Support Stack (electrolytes, amino complex, biotic, basic nutrients). This is the closest comp making exactly the AI-formulation move [[💡 X2|X2]] is built around — and GLP-1 Support is one of X2's priority formulations. Watch closely.
- Industry framing shifting from "products" to "personalized pathways" — cohort-specific formulations, GLP-1 companion products, healthspan focus dominate 2026 trend lists.

## AI in biotech
- The signal this morning is in consumer health, not drug discovery: Thorne's Taia is an AI-advisor-as-front-door play. The question for X2 is whether the moat is the advisor UX (Thorne's move) or the formulation engine underneath (X2's thesis).
