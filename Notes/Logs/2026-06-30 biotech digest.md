# 2026-06-30 — Biotech digest

## Top story
- **Ipsen to acquire Kartos Therapeutics for up to $1.75B** (announced 6/29), picking up navtemadlin, an MDM2 inhibitor in myelofibrosis. Mid-cap hematology M&A at a real premium — the deal market for de-risked rare-disease / onc assets is open, which is the exit environment Abio's pipeline ultimately plays into.

## Other items
- **AbbVie's $10.9B Apogee Therapeutics buy** (year's 2nd-largest biotech deal) still anchors the tape — large-cap pharma paying up to refill pipelines; biologics / I&I is where the money is flowing.
- **Funding is hot:** ~$450M across five private biotechs in one week, the busiest since April. Capital is back for the right stories.
- **⚡ Journal retracts a pivotal Amgen study** (Endpoints breaking alert) — reproducibility risk is a sentiment overhang for XBI; expect a headline drag.

## Nutraceutical / DTC watch
- **Thorne launched "Taia," an AI wellness advisor** that answers GLP-1 supplement questions and routes users to its GLP-1 Support Stack. GLP-1-claim products are up ~124% CAGR over five years (83% of that in North America). This is the AI-formulation-advisor concept sitting squarely in [[💡 X2|X2]]'s lane, anchored to X2's priority formulation.

## AI in biotech
- **Isomorphic Labs' IsoDDE** is posting ~50% accuracy on hard, low-homology (<20% similarity) protein–ligand structure cases vs AlphaFold 3's 23% — and is now in human trials with ~$3B in Lilly/Novartis deal value. **Recursion** has multiple 2026 readouts pending (REC-4881 showed polyp-burden reduction in 75% of FAP patients). >200 AI-designed candidates are now in the clinic.

## Signal for the week
- AI advisors are becoming table stakes in DTC supplements (Thorne's Taia) while AI structure engines (IsoDDE) cross the credibility threshold in drug design — the "AI + bio" narrative is compounding on both of Diogo's fronts at once.
