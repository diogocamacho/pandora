# 2026-06-29 — Biotech digest

Immunology + metabolic are where the bids are.

## AI in biotech
- **Pfizer licensed Chai Discovery's generative platform (Chai-3) (Jun 4)** for antibody/biomolecule design — big pharma is now *renting* computational design, not just building it. Chai-3 claims doubled hit rate, zero-shot.
- **Isomorphic Labs** closed a **$2.1B Series B** and shipped **IsoDDE** (50% accuracy on hard, low-homology protein–ligand cases vs AF3's 23%).
- Lane map consolidating into ~4: structure (Isomorphic), phenomics (Recursion), generative biologics (Xaira/Generate), antibody design (Chai).

## Signal for the week
Convergence is the meta-story: pharma renting AI *design* (Pfizer/Chai) and DTC renting AI *advice* (AG1/Thorne) hit both of Diogo's worlds in the same week. In each, the durable moat is the **proprietary feedback loop** — wet-lab active learning for Abio, per-user outcome data for [[💡 X2|X2]] — not the model or the marketing.
