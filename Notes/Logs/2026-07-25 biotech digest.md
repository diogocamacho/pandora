# 2026-07-25 biotech digest

## Top story
FDA granted Vera Therapeutics accelerated approval for **Trutakna (atacicept)** in IgA nephropathy (proteinuria reduction in adults at risk of progression). Atacicept is an engineered fusion-protein biologic — another market data point that **non-antibody designed biologics are clearing FDA**, which is the modality-validation signal that matters most for Abio.

## Other items
- **Gedatolisib (Revtorpyk)** approved for HR+/HER2− breast cancer, with fulvestrant ± palbociclib (7/14).
- **Pembrolizumab + enfortumab vedotin** cleared for muscle-invasive bladder cancer, peri-operative (7/10). The ADC + IO combo march continues.
- **Pharvaris deucrictibant** IR capsule NDA accepted for hereditary angioedema.
- **Biogen diranersen** — Phase 2 CELIA data showed tau reduction and cognitive benefit in early Alzheimer's. A real readout in a graveyard indication; the question is Phase 3 durability.
- **Novo Nordisk denecimig** — long-term safety data in hemophilia A.

## Nutraceutical / DTC watch
- **Thorne launched "Taia," an AI wellness advisor** that fields questions like "what should I take on a GLP-1?", paired with a four-product **GLP-1 Support Stack** (Basic Nutrients 2/Day, Complete Biotic, Amino Complex, Daily Electrolytes). This is the closest comp shipping exactly the **AI-formulation + GLP-1 support** combination X2 / [[💡 X2|Agentic Nutrition]] is building toward — sets the product bar and a pricing/positioning benchmark. Watch closely.
- Broader trend: the industry is moving off one-size-fits-all toward personalized, cohort-specific formulations, and a distinct "GLP-1 companion nutrition" category is forming (fiber, protein/amino, electrolytes, berberine).

## AI in biotech
- **Kalshi** opened prediction markets on clinical-trial outcomes and FDA approvals — a live, priced sentiment layer on binary biotech events. New, and likely to seep into how readouts get discussed.
- OpenFold consortium shipped a new **OpenFold3 model ("OpenBind,"** extended PDB cutoff 2025-06-30) in early access — relevant to Abio's OpenFold usage.

## Signal for the week
GLP-1 is now reshaping two of Diogo's worlds at once: it's the hottest supplement-design category (Thorne's move) and a driver of the metabolic-drug pipeline. The AI-personalization layer is where X2 has to differentiate before the incumbents (Thorne first) close the gap.
