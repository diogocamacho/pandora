## 🧭 [[Leadership Principles]]

The throughline across Leadership Journey, New Manager Foundations, Leveraging Your Leadership, and the 🦅 Leadership notes.

**The core arc**: self → others → organization. You cannot lead a team you don't understand, and you cannot lead an organization without a team that trusts you. Most technical leaders plateau at Expert or Achiever. The leverage move is Achiever → Strategist: from "I am the answer" to "I build the conditions for the answer."

**What actually sticks:**
- Listen as a leadership instrument, not politeness — it gives your team voice
- Trust is built in small, consistent actions and lost in one
- Be decisive in the decision; be inclusive in gathering input; own the result
- Your first job is making your people successful (servant leadership is practical)
- Develop a lieutenant — someone who sees what you can't from your seat
- Follow through relentlessly — this is the most common failure mode
- Candor over comfort; specificity over vagueness in feedback
- Change starts with modeling it yourself

**Where to grow**: the friction point from Achiever to Strategist is letting go of being the expert in the room.

## ⚓️ [[Flagship Academy Synthesis]]

Synthesized from the full November 2025 Academy week + December Multi-Product Platforms session.

**The Flagship frame**: discover a generative platform in unprecedented terrain, form a company around it, validate it step by step, extract multiple products from one foundational bet.

**Pioneering mindsets** (the ones that should change how you think):
- **Future-back**: never describe your innovation by contrasting with what exists today — take your audience to the destination first
- **Unreasonable**: if you can assess risk/benefit on a normal scale, it's probably not unreasonable enough
- **Consumer of uncertainty**: uncertainty is raw material, not risk to avoid — the biggest opportunities live outside the adjacency zone

**Platform fundamentals**:
- A platform = integrated system that generates *multiple* product options, not a single technology
- Programmability is the key differentiator: how cheaply can it adapt across applications?
- FAB framework: Capabilities → Advantages → Benefits
- Value = (size of opportunity) × (probability of success)
- Test any idea: generative? programmable? reusable? Where can you go from here?

**Traps**: premature scaling, fixating on the hardest problem, failure to update assumptions.

**Module 1 toolkit** (operator-level):
- Drug discovery: target druggability → validation → TPP → ADME/Tox. Platform success depends on where you aim it.
- IP: scope lives in the claims; tech companies file broad early, discovery companies protect outputs; always track FTO separately from your own exclusivity
- Financing: partnering is always dilutive; Flagship uses discount rates for the company, probabilities for programs
